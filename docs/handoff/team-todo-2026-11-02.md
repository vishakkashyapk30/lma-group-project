## Chosen architecture (3 Nov, provisional until the holdout run confirms it)
**c1457** = Full with four opt-in switches on (branch `arch-search`, results `docs/handoff/arch-search-results.md`):
- X1 `signal_source='merge'`: dossier first, rules on the raw facts fill what the dossier misses (custody period, parity, trial delay).
- X4 `abstention='strict'`: after repair, failing sentences and "the record is silent" sentences leave the argument and become open issues for counsel; an empty ground is not emitted.

- X7 `objection_grounding='by_id'`: Abhiyojak's objections are matched to the offered material by id, so valid objections are no longer discarded.

The 3 losses are structural (unfinished-factor share vs B4 and B5, confident-wrong vs B4) and are reported, not chased.
