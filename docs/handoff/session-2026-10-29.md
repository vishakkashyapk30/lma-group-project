## Measured on 2026-10-28/29 (Gemini free tier, the owner's key, Gemma 4 31B)
- The key lists 46 models. `gemma-4-31b-it` answers a JSON smoke request correctly in mode `fold+prompt+nothink`.
- Google's API is unstable: in the first hour of the full-system smoke 69 of 120 calls returned 500, 18 returned 503, 4 returned 429; 29 succeeded. On 2026-10-29 after the restart: 25 x 500, 3 x 503 in the first minutes. Failed calls are counted against the daily request ledger (conservative).
- Successful calls: about 126 answer tokens and about 1,000 thinking tokens each. With a 16K tokens-per-minute limit that is about 3 calls a minute. A Full case (about 47 calls) takes 15 minutes or more at best.
- Consequence: the sweep estimate in `gemini-free-tier.md` and `sweep-dev.md` (35,460 requests, about 3 days on one project) counted no failures and no thinking tokens, so it is **too optimistic**. At about 30 successes an hour the whole of phase 5 would take weeks. Re-plan before committing: shrink the sample sizes (not the features), or test `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` (no thinking problem, 500 requests a day, about 10 Full cases a day per project), or spread over teammates' projects.
