\section{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}

This section records the controls we applied against each kind of threat; Section~\ref{sec:limitations} is the complete list of what remains open.

\paragraph{Internal validity.} An effect smaller than its interval is reported as not resolved, and a win needs a Holm-adjusted paired-bootstrap interval that excludes zero. Two reruns on the development search orders (D12) keep c1457's three losses and change its wins by at most five cells, and an arm compared with its own rerun is decided in 2 of 225 cells. The architecture search chose c1457 on 30 development orders; the 20-order holdout and the sealed blind slice, opened once after the freeze tags and the registered hypotheses, guard against selection. The pass bar and the NLI threshold were fixed before the freeze, and margins are rounded to whole points. The one change to the blind scoring code, after a crash and before any score existed, is deviation D10, and a byte-identical rescoring of a scored development arm shows that every other sentence scores as before.

\paragraph{Construct validity.} Gold comes from rules and a model labeler that must agree on a verbatim span; the rule-only gold is the declared sensitivity analysis, and on the blind slice it changes no hypothesis verdict (Section~\ref{sec:blind}). The system reads the facts part of an order while gold is read from the other parts. The controls are an 8-word-overlap check between each test order's facts and every store paragraph, agreed labels, label-source sensitivity, and a template baseline (B6) that does not reuse the tagger. The answerable-factor rule and the faithful/corroborated table separate unsupported from wrong sentences, and lenient precision against G1 $\cup$ G5 credits factors that the court discussed. The unsupported-fact checker (MiniCheck) is independent of the system's own checker, which the scorer refuses to use.

\paragraph{External validity.} The study covers bail, with orders from one source, a native Hindi set from one High Court, and constructed data for T2 to T5, T7 and T8 whose answers are known by construction. The release date of the corpus bounds what a model could have seen.
