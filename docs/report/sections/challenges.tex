\section{Challenges and Mitigations}
\label{sec:challenges}

Table~\ref{tab:challenges} lists the problems we met while building and running the system, how we found each, and what we did about it. The largest were hosted-model quota, a privacy boundary that keeps some orders local, and gold labels that cover only some development orders; Section~\ref{sec:limitations} lists what stays open.

\toprule
Challenge & What happened & Mitigation \\
\midrule
Budget for hosted models & The plan needs about 4,500 Full case-runs; a Full case takes about 50 model calls. OpenAI was locked in code after its spend; Sarvam was locked, then opened on 1 November with an INR 5,000 cap that the ledger enforces. & Hosted runs moved to the Gemini free tier (500 requests a day per project per model), with a quota ledger and resumable shards; sample sizes were cut and no feature was; team keys are pooled. \\
Free-tier reliability & Gemma 4 31B failed on 86 of 94 calls in an hour (HTTP 500, 503); \texttt{gemini-3.5-flash-lite} rejected the default thinking setting with a bare 400. & Model chosen on a 3-case smoke (no failures on \texttt{gemini-3.5-flash-lite}); per-model thinking settings live in configuration; a case that hits the daily quota is not saved and reruns. \\
GPU serving & The account limit on job starts blocked serving Qwen3-32B on the shared GPU nodes. & The runs use hosted models; BGE-M3 vectors were built on the nodes (2\,h\,43\,min) and applied locally. \\
Thin input facts & The facts part of many orders is short or only a caption (19 of 50 dev orders under 1,000 characters). & Answerable-factor rule; coverage columns; cases with no facts are reported, not dropped. \\
Privacy refusals on hosted calls & The boundary kept some dev orders local (8 of the first 25; 26 of 100 baseline case-runs), so those cases saved an error and were scored as empty briefs. & The scorer counts errored cases explicitly, and every paired comparison uses only the cases both arms answered. \\
Sparse scorable gold & 8 of 50 dev orders have a gold factor that the facts make answerable and 20 have an agreed objection. & Every cell reports its number of cases; a 24-order set chosen from gold labels alone was used for the current-code runs; recall of cited precedents is measured in the offline studies and on the test slice. \\
Scoring time & MiniCheck runs on a laptop CPU: scoring one block of about 500 case-runs took over three hours. & Scoring runs unattended, one model family at a time; the larger blocks are scored for the final report. \\

Challenge & What happened & Mitigation \\
\midrule
Gold independence & The model labeler is the same model as the system in the OpenAI runs. & Rule-only gold is reported beside the agreed gold, and the blind runs use a different model family (Sarvam) from the labeler. \\
Checker circularity & The unsupported-fact metric first fell back to the system's own DeBERTa checker. & The scorer now uses MiniCheck and refuses the system's checker. \\
Privacy on the wire & Names were scrubbed but FIR numbers, police stations and dates went out. & Quasi-identifier aliasing and a per-case date shift; per-case consent for client text; free tiers take public text only. \\
Rights rules not verified & Rule texts were to be checked by hand against the bare acts, and the store held the wrong text for BNSS s.58. & A script checks each rule's cited sections against the statute store and sets the date only on a match; s.58 is now taken from the official Gazette. Seven of seven rules match, after the Companies Act s.212 was added to the store from the Ministry's text. Verification is bound to a hash of rule text and code; exports print ``Not yet checked against the bare act'' for any rule that is not verified. \\
Statute-store bail flags & After the runs we found that the First Schedule table was read the wrong way round for 43 of 398 IPC sections. & The schedule was re-extracted by script, the product reads it, and the store was rebuilt on 13 November. \\
Empty statutory position & The hosted extractor answered with the phrase of the order where the schema asks for one object per section, and every such answer was rejected. & A phrase is read with the rule parser, one section per value, each checked against its quote. \\
Maximum punishment for BNS 109 with 3(5) and NDPS clauses & The radar dropped the maximum of a charge that included a section with no punishment of its own, and found no row for an NDPS clause. & A section that carries only the offence's punishment is left out, and a quantity-band clause is read from the section text (21(a) 1 year, 21(b) 10 years, 21(c) 20 years); demo cases 03 and 05 now show real statuses. \\
Sparring token budget & Drafting used the token budget before most replies were judged (68\% of blind objections never judged). & An opt-in per-case reserve judges each reply right after it is drafted; the product runs it at 200{,}000 tokens, and an objection it cannot cover goes to counsel with a stated reason. On 30 development orders the judged share rose from 26\% to 97\%. \\
Gated data & HLDC (Hindi district-court bail orders) and an Indic translation model are gated. & Native Hindi High Court orders as T2's Hindi set; access requested. \\
\bottomrule
